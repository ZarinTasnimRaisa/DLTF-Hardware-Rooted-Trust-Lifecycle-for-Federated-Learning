Part 01 · Enrollment

One prior for every tier: Beta(1, 1)

v3 gives every device the same non-informative prior (W = 2, base rate a = 0.5), so every device starts at trust 0.5 regardless of tier. Tier no longer lives in the prior — it lives only in the influence discount D and in whether the device can be rehabilitated. Starting trust is an output of the prior, not a knob.

Part 02 · Strike rules P1–P3

Same offense, tier-coupled consequence — from the discount, not the prior

Fresh devices, identical Kang evidence weights (θ = 0.4 good, τ = 0.6 bad). The trust trajectories are identical across tiers; what differs is the consequence of crossing a threshold, set by rehab-eligibility (Tier 1 probates and can recover; Tiers 2–3 ban).

Part 03 · Mechanism M1

Bounded memory: goodwill saturates

Positive evidence decays by γ = 0.9 each round, so banked goodwill saturates at s_max = θ/(1−γ) = 4 and trust can never exceed 0.8333 — the same ceiling for every tier. A veteran cannot buy an unbounded attack budget.

Part 04 · Mechanism M2

Earned forgetting: penalties fade only after a sustained clean streak

One MAJOR at round 1, then clean rounds. The penalty f is frozen until 5 consecutive clean rounds, then fades at FORGET = 0.95 per round — half the rate goodwill decays (Slovic direction; magnitude calibrated). v3 applies this uniformly to every tier.

Part 05 · Mechanism M3 (ungated)

Sleeper defense: consecutive MAJORs are counted, not scored

40 clean rounds bank maximal goodwill, then the device turns. The trust score alone is still 0.6558 at the second MAJOR — above the 0.5 threshold — but the consecutive-MAJOR rule escalates anyway. M3 is ungated: it fires even while the u-gate protects the score path.

Part 06 · Mechanism (new in v3) · the u-gate

Cold-start protection: no score sanction while the prior dominates

The subjective-logic uncertainty is u = W/(W+s+f). While u ≥ 0.5 (equivalently s+f ≤ W = 2) the opinion is still prior-dominated, so score-path sanctions are withheld. Left: a single early noise MINOR is absorbed (the gate stays shut until round 6). Right: a genuine all-MINOR attacker pushes the gate open at round 4 and is sanctioned at round 4. M3 and CRITICAL bypass the gate entirely.

Part 07 · Thresholds

The trust line and its three cut-points

All score transitions live on one axis. 0.5 is the subjective-logic indifference point (and Kang's detection cut). 0.75 = 0.5·(1+1/W) is the reinstatement floor. 0.3 is a backstop that, under D5, an active rehab-eligible device never reaches directly — probation comes first.

Part 08 · Influence

Trust becomes aggregation weight through the tier discount

weight = D[tier] × trust (Jøsang discounting; weight-by-reputation per Kang / RepuNet). This is now the only place tier enters the numbers. Probation forces any suspect down to the unproven-identity cap of 0.1 regardless of tier.

Part 09 · Reinstatement

The trial is evidence — but with W = 2 the floor dominates

Three probated Tier-1 devices reinstated with 0 / 6 / 9 credited trial rounds. Everyone clears the 0.75 floor.

FLAGGED DEVIATION · REINSTATE_T ↔ W coupling With W = 2 the reinstatement floor requires more evidence (need_s = (r/(1−r))(b₀+f) − a₀ ≥ 3.8 for any f ≥ τ) than the maximum trial credit θ·W_MAX = 0.4×9 = 3.6 can ever supply. So every reinstatement returns at exactly 0.75, whatever the trial length — the v2.1 property “stronger recovery returns higher” is retired. Direction is favourable (recovery is never too generous); it is surfaced, not hidden.
